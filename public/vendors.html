Ordering tips for service advisors
Insurance vs customer-pay
Insurance jobs typically require OEM or CAPA-certified aftermarket. Always confirm with the adjuster before ordering non-OEM. Customer-pay gives you more flexibility — discuss options and cost difference with the customer.
Cross-referencing part numbers
Look up the OEM part number in Mitchell 1 ProDemand first. Then use that number to search Worldpac or NAPA — they often have the identical part at lower cost. Always verify fitment by year/make/model/VIN.
When parts are backordered
Flag the RO as "Waiting parts" and note the ETA in the comment thread. Check Car-Part.com or LKQ for a used OEM alternative. Call the customer proactively — don't wait for them to call you.
Core charges
Alternators, starters, AC compressors, and brake calipers usually have a core charge. Hold the old part until the new one arrives. Return the core same day when possible — most vendors only waive the charge if returned promptly.
Warranty claims
Always get the receipt and part number in the RO. If a warranty part fails, you need the original invoice to file. NAPA ProLink and Worldpac both have online warranty claim portals — know your account rep.
Documenting in the RO
Add every part to the parts line in the RO before ordering — include vendor name, part number, and cost. This feeds the burn rate dashboard and ensures accurate invoicing. Never order a part without it being in the RO first.
